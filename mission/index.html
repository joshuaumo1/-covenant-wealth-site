Proposed three-year launch path

Build credibility before scale.

A founding roadmap for development, rather than a report of completed milestones.

Year 1 · Foundation

Prove the model

  • Form the entity, board, policies, and core team.
  • Develop or license curriculum and launch the platform.
  • Pilot training cohorts and the membership community.
  • Document outcomes, partner roles, and delivery standards.
Year 2 · Growth

Expand the offer

  • Add one-to-one and group coaching pathways.
  • Host a partner conference or showcase.
  • Formalize church, college, and business partnerships.
  • Build scholarship fundraising and improve retention.
Year 3 · Scale

Multiply reach

  • Grow memberships, courses, and certified coaches.
  • Launch investor-readiness and referral pathways.
  • Pilot local hubs or licensed delivery partners.
  • Institutionalize quality, succession, reserves, and cash flow.
