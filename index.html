Partnership ecosystem

Partners accelerate reach while entrepreneurs receive a trusted pathway.

Churches, schools, coaches, business leaders, investors, donors, and community organizations each have a defined role. The goal is not one-time activity; it is a repeatable pathway with shared outcomes.

Host

Convene a pilot cohort or partner event.

Refer

Nominate entrepreneurs, mentors, and coaches.

Sponsor

Fund scholarships, curriculum, or capacity.

Advise

Shape programs, governance, and outcomes.

Launch discipline

Build credibility before scale.

Year 1 | Foundation

Prove the model

  • Form the entity, board, policies, and core team.
  • Pilot training cohorts and the membership community.
  • Document outcomes and delivery standards.
Year 2 | Growth

Expand the offer

  • Add one-to-one and group coaching pathways.
  • Host the first partner showcase.
  • Formalize church, college, and business partnerships.
Year 3 | Scale

Multiply reach

  • Grow memberships, courses, and certified coaches.
  • Launch investor-readiness and referral pathways.
  • Pilot local hubs or licensed delivery partners.
